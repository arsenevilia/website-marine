Who we are

Commercial Efficiency & Reliable Protection

Operating in the maritime industry since 2011, our team brings together deep expertise in both commercial shipping and security operations. We go beyond traditional brokerage by seamlessly connecting shipowners and charterers, while simultaneously bridging the gap between the shipping community and private security providers.

Security and smooth commercial execution are among the most critical challenges for modern global shipping. We have developed a trusted network of cooperation with shipowners and respected Private Maritime Security Companies (PMSCs), allowing us to offer competitive solutions in accordance with all global requirements—including the Gulf of Guinea, Somalia HRA, Southeast Asia, and other sensitive transit zones.

We ensure the presence of highly experienced operatives and the deployment of authorized, compliant weapons and equipment, guaranteeing safe passage without costly delays or diversions. Coordinating the work of experienced security teams, we ensure their seamless integration with the ship's crew, strict compliance with flag state regulations, and full alignment with P&I Club requirements.

Maritor also provides specialized marine risk management and insurance arrangement services for vessels operating in war zones and High Risk Areas (HRAs). Leveraging our established international market network, we secure tailored War & Related Risks coverage to protect our principals’ commercial interests in the world’s most volatile waters.
