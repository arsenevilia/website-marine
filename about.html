Who we are

Commercial Efficiency & Reliable Protection

Operating in the maritime industry since 2011, our team combines hands-on expertise in commercial shipping, marine insurance, and maritime security operations. We go beyond traditional brokerage by seamlessly connecting shipowners and charterers, while bridging the operational gap between the shipping community, leading insurance markets, and vetted Private Maritime Security Companies (PMSCs).

On the commercial side, we broker dry bulk tonnage, matching reliable vessels with cargo requirements to structure practical, commercially sound fixtures.

At the same time, security and uninterrupted execution remain vital to modern shipping. Through our established network of accredited PMSCs, we coordinate seasoned armed security teams equipped with authorized, properly licensed firearms and gear across high-risk corridors—including the Red Sea, Gulf of Aden, Indian Ocean, Gulf of Guinea, and Southeast Asia—ensuring smooth crew integration and strict compliance with flag state and P&I Club requirements.

Complementing our chartering and security operations, Maritor provides specialized marine risk management and insurance placement services for vessels operating in war zones and High-Risk Areas (HRAs). Leveraging direct access to international insurance markets, we arrange specialized marine and war risk coverage protecting commercial interests in the world’s most turbulent waters.
