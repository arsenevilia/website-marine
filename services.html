Brokerage & Chartering

Operating worldwide across the dry bulk sector, our team provides the market insights and professional expertise needed to navigate the industry. Whether you are looking for a vessel for your cargo or a cargo for your vessel, we secure the best options for you—handling the complete fixture process from initial negotiations and Charter Party management to full voyage execution, safeguarding our principals' commercial interests at every stage.

Armed Maritime Security & Escort Vessels

Coordinating maritime protection for vessels transiting the world's most critical high-risk corridors, including the Red Sea, Gulf of Aden, Bab el-Mandeb, Indian Ocean. Through our established network of vetted PMSCs, we arrange certified, experienced Armed Security Teams equipped with authorized, properly licensed firearms and gear, ensuring smooth crew integration and strict compliance with flag state and P&I Club requirements. In West Africa and the Gulf of Guinea, where local legislation restricts private armed guards, we arrange Host Nation Naval Armed Guards and specialized Security Escort Vessels. Backed by strict vetting and clear operational coordination, we safeguard your vessels, crews, and assets at every stage of the voyage.

Marine Insurance & War Risk

Working with international insurance markets, we structure tailored coverage for commercial vessels and charterers.
